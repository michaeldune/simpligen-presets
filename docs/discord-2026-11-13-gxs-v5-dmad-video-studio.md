**Now in the Video Studio: MiniMax H3 (DaSiWa GXS v5) and MiniMax H3 (Hybrid DMAD PDMD)**
Both packs are at 1.0.3, and all six cards (text, image and reference to video in each pack) can now be picked as the MiniMax H3 build for a shot in the Video Studio. If you already have either pack, the store will offer the update. The models and workflows are unchanged, so there is nothing new to download.

**What we checked**
We filmed a three-shot film on each pack in the Studio at 480p: a words-only shot, a shot that continues from it, and a cast member in a saved location with one spoken line.
- Every shot ran on the card that was picked, with that pack's own checkpoint.
- The spoken line came out word for word on both packs, and the character stayed recognisable.
- A five-second shot took about 85 to 100 s on each of the three cards, on both packs.

**Worth knowing**
A five-second continue shot may not get through everything you ask of it. In our test the train was meant to reach a tunnel and was still on the viaduct at the end. Give a continue shot one change, or make the clip longer.

Measured on a 12 GB RTX 4070 Ti with Faster Attention on.
