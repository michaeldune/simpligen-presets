**pi-H3 2-Step now does reference to video (pack 1.1.0)**
The MiniMax H3 (pi-H3 2-Step) pack has a third card: reference to video. The model card does not mention it, but the same two-step adapter also works on the standard REF2VA weights. Thanks to Photogenic Weekend for showing it working.

**What you need**
Nothing new if you already have pi-H3 and the standard H3 reference weights: the card uses the same 2.2 GB adapter. Otherwise the app downloads the REF2VA model when you install the preset. Like the other H3 reference cards, it takes up to nine pictures, three clips and three voices.

**Speed** (in the app, 12 GB RTX 4070 Ti, Faster Attention on, 5 s at 480p)
- pi-H3 2-step: 36 to 37 s.
- MATLOW's fused 4-step turbo on the same shot: about 50 s.
- pi-H3's 4-step adapter: also about 50 s, so the card stays at two steps.

**What we checked**
- One reference picture and a scripted line, on two different people: face, hair, outfit and jewellery carried over, and the line came out word for word both times.
- Sol-Attn stays off, as on the other two cards, because it smears small faces with this model.

All three pi-H3 cards are in the Video Studio, so the reference card works for cast shots there. In the Preset Store after the next sync.
