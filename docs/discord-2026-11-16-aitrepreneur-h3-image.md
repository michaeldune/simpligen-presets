**Video: Aitrepreneur on MiniMax H3-Image**
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RK0Wg235kdE (17 min)

After a week with it, he calls H3-Image the best high-resolution editing model he has used. It is MiniMax's 33B image model, which does both text to image and editing with up to 16 reference pictures.

**His two main tips**
- **Render big.** He says the model was trained above 16 megapixels, so low-resolution renders are why some people were let down. His 16 MP render took about 32 s on his card. For a group shot of four women in a living room he went to 48 MP. He says the faces and details got much better, though it takes a few minutes.
- **Prompt in detail.** Name the lens and depth of field, and add words like "candid photography", or let the prompt writer expand a short line. He says most bad results come from the prompt, not the model.

**What he shows**
- Changing a hairstyle while the rest of the photo stays the same
- Putting a person from one photo into a new scene, with face and clothes kept
- Four people plus a room photo combined into one picture
- Painting in glasses, and painting in a jacket and a water bottle taken from two other photos
- Widening a portrait to 16:9 with no visible seam

He says anime works but isn't the model's strength. His workflow and installer are on his Patreon, and his prompt writer is an uncensored Qwen 3.5 build.
