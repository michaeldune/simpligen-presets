:thread: **New pack: Outfit Spec Sheet** (1 card, Qwen Image Edit)

You may have seen the "turn any outfit photo into a fashion design character sheet" prompt going round for ChatGPT. This is the part of it a local model can actually do: one photo of someone wearing an outfit in, a lookbook-style board out - the garments and accessories lifted off the person onto a mannequin against warm ivory, soft shadow, and a strip of three close-up detail crops. Deliberately no text and no colour swatches: local edit models garble captions and invent swatch colours (tested, both Qwen and Klein), and a clean caption-free board is the thing you hand to an illustrator or feed into another generator anyway.

The brief is baked into the card, so the prompt box is just for extra notes ("keep the sunglasses", "show the back"). Same weights as SimpliGen's own Image Edit pack, so if you have that installed nothing downloads. About 40-80 s per board on a 4070 Ti. Tested on a coat-and-trousers look, a printed wrap dress, a three-piece suit and a single biker jacket: every garment and accessory came through. It draws a plain white mannequin rather than an invisible one, and it can occasionally invent a button in a close-up.
