**New pack: Krea 2 Art & Styles**
Stock Krea 2 Turbo already does anime, painting and fantasy prompts well, so a style checkpoint only earns its 13 GB if it gives you a different picture. We ran six Krea 2 checkpoints side by side against stock Turbo on the same prompts and seeds, and these four did.

The cards:
- **Equinox (Semi-Realistic)**: crisp, idealised semi-real characters that compose their own picture instead of repeating stock Krea 2's. Good for anime, fantasy and illustration. It restyles photo prompts too (younger, glossier), so it is not a photo card.
- **Fascium KREA ART (Painterly)**: 99 painterly artist styles merged into one checkpoint. It turns everything into paint. Call a style by name: "in the manner of Rembrandt", "in the style of Gustav Klimt", "in the manner of Alphonse Mucha", "Piranesi style". SFW by design.
- **Fwaun Tune (Semi-Chibi)**: flat colour, no outlines, softly big-headed characters. A strong style: it redrew a photo prompt and a Rembrandt prompt this way too. The card adds the "semi-chibi style." trigger for you.
- **Wulver (Anthro)**: a full fine-tune for anthro, furry and kemono characters. Where stock Krea 2 drew a small anime cat-wolf, Wulver drew a real wolf-headed character, three times out of three. The card adds the "sfw," rating tag the model expects.

Good to know:
- All four render at about 2 MP (1216x1632 and equivalents) in 25-45 seconds on a 12 GB card, with the same Upscale slider and LoRA slot as the other Krea 2 cards.
- Wulver knows 1,113 artist tokens (@name, typed first; the list is on the model's Hugging Face page). Two catches we saw on every test: a token can write the artist's name on the picture, and the character can lose clothing you asked for.
- Fascium KREA ART is not an anime card: an anime prompt comes back as a painted illustration.
- Tested but left out: DaSiWa Krea2 Turbo and Krea 2 Anime UWU. Both are fine models, but on our prompts they stayed close to stock Krea 2 Turbo or to the Moody mix we already ship.
- For photography use the Krea 2 or Krea 2 Finetunes packs.

Each card downloads only its own 13-14 GB checkpoint; the text encoder and VAE are shared with the other Krea 2 packs. Krea 2 Community License, plus each author's terms on their model page.
