**Two new MiniMax H3 packs: Heptagram v2 Entropy and PRISM-PIKA**
Both came out of this month's speed and quality test. Each has Text, Image and Reference cards with the speed LoRAs built in, so there is nothing else to load. Neither one replaces a card you already use. They are two different looks.

**PRISM-PIKA** (OliviaRossi)
A merge of Singularity v2 and 10Eros Max with Pika H3 Flex built in at 0.85.
- Looks close to Pika Flex with stronger light, and runs about 20 seconds quicker.
- About 60 s for 5 s at 480p, 70 s for Image and Reference, and 2 3/4 minutes at 768p.
- Held a locked-off shot and kept a quiet room quiet.
- The author gives no settings, so the cards use Pika Flex's six-step schedule. That choice is ours.
- Do not add a speed LoRA. Pika warn that a second accelerator breaks the output.
- 18.8 GB.

**Heptagram v2 Entropy** (eatyet)
The new version of Heptagram, and still a preview. The author says the final form depends on community feedback.
- High contrast, dense detail and big expressions. Good for punch, but stylised faces can look overdone.
- About 80 s for 5 s at 480p, 85-90 s for Image and Reference, and 3 1/2 minutes at 768p.
- **It does not hold a still shot.** Use another card for a locked-off camera.
- **Sound runs very loud,** about 16 dB above stock on busy prompts. A quiet-room prompt came back with a constant low rumble, so pick another card if you want near silence.
- 8 steps by default. The Steps control covers the author's 4 to 8, and only 8 was tested.
- 19.5 GB, separate from Heptagram Turbo.

On both, first and last frames landed and two references held on all three seeds of our test. Both are tagged Mature. Measured on a 12 GB RTX 4070 Ti with 32 GB of RAM, engine v0.43.0.
