**Qwen Image 2.1 pack 1.8.0: new Edit (Steady Frame) card**
Plain Qwen Image 2.1 edits drift. Ask for a watercolor or a comic version and the picture often comes back a few percent taller or nudged sideways, so it no longer lines up with your original. The new Steady Frame card is the Edit card plus ausboss's Consistency LoRA (159 MB), which makes the edit on the original's frame.

What we measured (14 edits on three pictures, two seeds each, compared with the plain Edit card):
- Watercolor restyles: the worst corner moved about 5 px instead of about 44 px.
- Recolours, sign changes and a head turn: 0.1 px instead of up to 7 px.
- The look of the edit didn't change, and moves you ask for (a head turn, a cat in her arms) still happen.
- Two- and four-picture edits work the same as on the Edit card.

Good to know:
- Same pictures and wording as the Edit card: refer to them as <image1>, <image2> and so on.
- About a third slower with one picture (around 2 1/4 minutes instead of 1 3/4 on a 12 GB card); about the same with several.
- Use it when you want to lay the edit over the original: restyles, recolours, relights, small changes. The original Edit card is unchanged.

Only the 159 MB LoRA downloads if you already have the pack. Non-commercial use only (Qwen Research License).
