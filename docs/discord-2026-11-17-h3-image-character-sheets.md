New community pack: **MiniMax H3-Image Character Sheets** (9 cards)

One card per layout from ukr8b3g-cmyk's H3 Character Sheet Designer for ComfyUI, so you pick the sheet you want instead of building it. Give it one picture of your character, leave the prompt empty (or add a note like "bare feet" or "anime style"), and you get a 16-megapixel sheet on a white background:

- Five Views: front and side portraits, full body front, side and back
- Four Views: front portrait, full body front, side and back
- Turnaround: full body front, side and back
- Full Body: one clean standing view, handy for cutting a character out of a busy scene
- Six Head Angles: front, both profiles, both three-quarters, back of the head
- Seven Views + Hands and Feet: Five Views plus close-ups of the hands and footwear
- Outfit Front and Back: the clothes laid flat, nobody wearing them
- All Thirteen Views: head angles, full body, flat outfit, hands and feet on one sheet
- Animal or Mascot: keeps fur, scales and markings instead of dressing the character

Each card has its own fixed canvas, so the aspect-ratio setting does nothing. About 70-90 seconds per sheet on a 12 GB card. It uses the same model files as the MiniMax H3-Image pack, with no prompt writer, and needs the beta engine (ComfyUI 0.45.0) like that pack does.

One honest note: Outfit Front and Back is the fussiest of the nine. About half the time it draws a garment twice or shortens a long coat, so give it a second seed. The other eight came out clean on nearly every run in our tests (a photo, an anime drawing and two creatures).
