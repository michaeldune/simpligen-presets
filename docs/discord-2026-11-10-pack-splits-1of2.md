**Preset Store update: big community packs split up**
Four community packs had grown to 13-28 cards, so I've split them into packs of 8 or fewer. No cards were removed and no settings changed; the cards just moved.

**If you update one of these packs, install the new ones too, or the moved cards drop out of your list.** Models you already have stay on disk, so the installs are quick. The new pack names also drop the "Community —" prefix.

**Krea 2**
- **Krea 2 Realism:** the photoreal cards. It's the old Krea 2 pack, renamed so it no longer shares a name with SimpliGen's own Krea 2.
- **Krea 2 Anime** (new): the anime cards from Art & Styles, plus VXP Beta 4 and DaSiWa DarkDesire.
- **Krea 2 Art & Styles:** the style cards, plus BF95 Dark Realism and VXP Beta 3.

**Qwen Image 2.1**
- **Qwen Image 2.1:** text to image, plus Edit and Fast Edit.
- **Qwen Image 2.1 Edit Tools** (new): Steady Frame, Edit 4MP, Enhance to 4MP, Cutout, Character Sheet, Head Swap, Outfit Swap and Pose Studio.

**Illustrious**
- **Illustrious Anime:** the 7 plain anime cards.
- **Illustrious Art & Styles** (new): UnrealVision, RIN Flanime, One Obsession v24, Rule of Moon, PerfectDeliberate and SmoothMix Ultimate.

**Anima**
- **Anima Anime:** the official models and the fast cards (Base, Turbo, 2.9B preview, VXP Turbo, RDBT, Diving).
- **Anima Large** (new): the fine-tunes on the bigger 2.9B and 3.8B models.
- **Anima Anime Mixes** (new): Ri-Mix, One Obsession v4 and v5, CocoaMix, Unholy Desire, Element 7 and Male GG Mix.
- **Anima Art & Styles** (new): MiaoMiao, Pie Cream, Riaku Lab, Pearly Unknown and MyGoo.

A few of the moved cards may not run in the cloud until they're re-approved. Running them on your own PC works now.
