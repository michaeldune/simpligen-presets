**15 Video Studio presets, one vampire**
After watching Daisy Studios test 14 AI video generators, I ran the same kind of talking-head test on 15 of our Video Studio presets, all rendered locally.

**Setup:** one picture of Anastasia, one 13-second voice clip, one 32-word line, seed 4242, 15 seconds at 480p. The 8+2 cards only do 10 seconds, so those ran 10 s at 768p. Scored out of 50 on picture, face and fangs, lip sync, acting, and following the prompt.

**Top five**
1. PRISM DMAD PDMD - 41
2. 10Eros beta7 fix1 - 39
3. Official H3, PRISM-PIKA, Heptagram v2 - 38 each

The rest: Midnight 36, DaSiWa v5 36, Hybrid DMAD 35, 8+2 DenoMix 35, 8+2 Singularity 34, Pika Flex 32, REDgraft 31, Turbo Accelerated 31, LTX 2.5 30, Stubelius 30.

**What stood out**
- Every build said every word. Only Pika Flex repeated part of the line.
- Fangs were the real test, because they aren't in the picture. Heptagram had the best ones, PRISM DMAD, PRISM-PIKA and 10Eros showed them too, and the LTX cards never did.
- Hands varied a lot. Hybrid DMAD kept them on her hips almost the whole clip; PRISM DMAD gestured the most.
- Loudness swung by 12 dB. The cards with no voice input (Turbo Accelerated and the three LTX cards) came out loudest. I left the audio as rendered.
- Render times on a 4070 Ti 12 GB, model load included: LTX 1.5 to 2.5 min, PRISM-PIKA 4 min, most H3 cards 5 to 6 min, official H3 8 min, the 8+2 cards about 9 min.
