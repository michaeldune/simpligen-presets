**Anima Large 1.0.3: Pawhibited card retired**

The Pawhibited Anima 2.9B v1.0 card is gone from the Anima Large pack. The model and its author's Civitai account (CatgirlContraband) have been deleted, so the file can't be downloaded anymore. Since yesterday evening the store has been holding Anima Large at the old version because of it.

- **Already have Pawhibited installed?** Your model file stays on your disk. The card is kept in the repo under `packs/anima-large/retired/`, with steps to add it back by hand.
- **Never installed it?** There's no download source left. comfy.icu and civitaiarchive still list it, but their download links go to the deleted Civitai file.
- **The other seven Anima Large cards are unchanged:** Nova Orange, Nova Anime, Delta Mix v7 Flat, KodoraAN, CC-Dream Island Mature, One Obsession 2.9B v2 and Delta Mix 3.8B V8.

1.0.3 should show up in the store after the next community sync.
