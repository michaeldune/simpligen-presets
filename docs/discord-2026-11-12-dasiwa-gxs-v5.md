**New pack: MiniMax H3 (DaSiWa GXS v5)**
Darksidewalker's newest DaSiWa checkpoint, GXS v5 Turbo, requested here this week. Three cards: text, image and reference to video, all on one 19.5 GB file with the distillation built in, so there is no Turbo LoRA and nothing extra to install.

**What it is**
The author describes it as a merge of DaSiWa v4, DaSiWa v5 and Singularity v2. It is a new pack beside the v1 to v4 packs, which stay as they are.

**Steps**
- The cards default to 8 steps, the author's quality setting.
- The slider goes down to 4. The author calls 4 a fast preview with weaker audio and 6 the in-between.
- In the app on a 12 GB card at 480p, a five-second shot takes about 85 to 90 s for text to video at 8 steps (about 50 s at 4), and about 90 to 120 s for image and reference shots.

**What we checked**
- A scripted line came out word for word on three of three seeds at 4, 6 and 8 steps.
- Every identity held on a solo reference, a two-person shot and a person-plus-location set, each over three seeds.
- On one seed in three, a person-plus-location shot opened on the person's own photo for about a second and then dissolved into the scene. Try another seed if you see that.

**Download**
Needs a Civitai API key in Settings: the author requires sign-in to download. The model itself is free. If you already have another H3 pack, only the 19.5 GB checkpoint is new.

Measured on a 12 GB RTX 4070 Ti with Faster Attention on.
Thanks to Darksidewalker for the checkpoint.
