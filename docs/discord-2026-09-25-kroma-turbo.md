**Krea 2 Finetunes (1.3.0): new Kroma v0.3 Turbo card** :underage: NSFW
lodestones' Kroma is Krea 2 trained further on the Chroma dataset, from the maker of Chroma. It has more artistic range than stock Krea 2 and it is uncensored out of the box. This card uses silveroxides' INT8 version of the v0.3 "txtfusion" turbo checkpoint (14.1 GB; the encoder and VAE are shared with the other Krea 2 cards).
Tips from testing:
- In our tests it matched FinalCut on 15 of 15 pictures: clothing colours, three described people in one scene, shop signs and a small window card, and anime line art. It is also a bit faster, about 25 seconds for a 1408x1408 picture on a 12 GB card.
- CFG is fixed at 1 on purpose: higher CFG breaks the picture up into speckle, so there is no CFG slider.
- The Upscale slider works the same as on the other cards.
The other four cards are unchanged. Krea 2 community licence for the base weights.
