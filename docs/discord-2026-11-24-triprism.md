**New pack: MiniMax H3 (TriPrism)**
OliviaRossi's TriPrism is her newest H3 merge: Sulphur 3, 10Eros Max and Singularity v2, with the DMAD and PDMD distillations and a realism LoRA baked into one file. The pack has three cards: text, image and reference to video.

**What you need**
One 21.1 GB model file, downloaded from Hugging Face when you install the preset, so no Civitai key is needed. Nothing else to install, and no LoRA to load. Eight steps by default (euler, simple, no CFG). The model card gives no settings, so the cards use the same ones as PRISM DMAD PDMD.

**How it compares** (in the app, 12 GB RTX 4070 Ti, Faster Attention on, 5 s)
Over seven seeds against PRISM DMAD PDMD int8 on the same prompts, it made the same shots with slightly warmer colour, and it was faster every time:
- Text to video, 480p: 72 s against 90 s.
- Reference to video, 480p: 82 s against 96 s.
- Text to video, 768p: 216 s against 240 s.

**What we checked**
- Scripted lines came out word for word on the reference card, and the person kept their face, hair and clothes.
- Image to video starts on your picture and plays out the action as written.

All three cards are coming to the Video Studio. In the Preset Store after the next sync.
