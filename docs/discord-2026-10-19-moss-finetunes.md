**New pack: MOSS-Image Finetunes - two Civitai finetunes for MOSS-Image (beta engine for now)**
Two cards built on community finetunes of MOSS-Image: **Sam_MOSS Turbo** and **Moody Moss Mix**. We ran five MOSS finetunes from Civitai side by side against the MOSS-Image pack on nine prompts (six posters, three photos, same seed), and these two earned a place:
- **Sam_MOSS Turbo** (toya_san): the speed is built in, 8 steps, no LoRA. Skin and light look natural rather than retouched, and it keeps the light and mood you ask for, so for portraits and photo-style prompts it is the fast card to pick over MOSS-Image Fast.
- **Moody Moss Mix** (catlover1937): 25 steps at CFG 3. Deeper contrast and richer colour than MOSS-Image, and painterly when you ask for illustration.

Like the MOSS-Image pack it needs the **beta engine** for now: **Settings > Updates > beta**, take the engine update, and the pack installs. It opens on stable by itself once a tagged engine release carries MOSS.

Measured on a 12 GB RTX 4070 Ti:
- **Sam_MOSS Turbo:** about 12 s per picture.
- **Moody Moss Mix:** about 27 s per picture.
- Every poster's quoted headline came out spelled right on both cards.

Good to know:
- **Put every word you want on the picture in quotes**, same as MOSS-Image.
- Sam_MOSS Turbo dropped a word from one small label in our test ("17 Pro" instead of "iPhone 17 Pro"), so check small print.
- They share the MOSS-Image encoder and autoencoder: with the MOSS-Image pack installed, each card adds about 5 GB.
- The three we left out: Moss Image Turbo and Artius Moss gave smooth, retouched skin and followed prompts less closely; PornMaster Moss looked almost the same as MOSS-Image on our SFW prompts.
- Licences are the authors' Civitai terms: Sam_MOSS allows selling the pictures you make; Moody Moss Mix lists only Civitai's own generation service, so check its page before commercial use.
