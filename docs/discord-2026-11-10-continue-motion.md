**Continue the movement** works on the LTX 2.5 community cards too (tested REDgraft; Stubelius uses the same graph). **Continue from the end of** works on the H3 community cards (tested Pika Flex): the new clip starts on the exact last frame, so the background and framing carry over. The camera move and speed only carry over if the prompt asks for them, so describe the same move in both clips.

@fsanders about Generate being greyed out on LTX 2.5: one thing that does that is a cast with no location. LTX 2.5 builds cast shots from the reference pictures and won't render a cast without a place to stand, so pick a location in the cast or take the cast off that clip. For continue motion, leave the cast off the second clip anyway. If that's not it, post a screenshot of the clip panel and we'll look.
