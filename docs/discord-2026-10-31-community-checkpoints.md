**Eight new community-requested checkpoint cards**
These came from a list of 12 checkpoints posted here. Each one was tested side by side against its base model, then run in the app. They reach the Preset Store with the next sync; update the pack to get them.

- **Anima Anime 1.7.0**
  - **Pawhibited v1.0** (Meiyue with CatgirlContraband): on the 2.9B base, with dramatic light and closer framing. About 27 s.
  - **CocoaMix Anima v4.0** (ccooccoo): clean, soft and bright. About 22 s. It draws no pupils unless you add `dark circle pupils`, and pictures are non-commercial unless you agree otherwise with the author.
- **MOSS-Image Finetunes 1.2.0** (beta engine for now, like the MOSS-Image pack)
  - **Moss Serenity v1.0** (malcolmrey): brighter and flatter, with looser lettering. About 23 s.
  - **AniMOSSity v1.0** (lonecatone23): the deepest colour and contrast, and the strongest posters in our test. It only comes as a 10 GB bf16 file, so about 53 s.
  - **MOSS Cat Tower v1.0** (nuko_masshigura): the most polished anime of the MOSS cards, on the author's recipe of 50 steps with Skip Layer Guidance. About 34 s. Needs engine v0.43.0 or later.
- **Krea 2 Art & Styles 1.3.0**
  - **Animosity v2.0** (lonecatone23): dark, ornate, heavily coloured semi-real anime, and the version with the fix for same-looking faces. About 28 s at 2 MP.
- **SDXL Art & Anime 1.3.0**
  - **Gock So Anime Love Song** (GockSo): warm, glowing anime with close framing. About 13 s.
- **Pony Anime 1.2.0**
  - **Osorubeshi-Ultra Anime-V1** (OsorubeshiAI): bright, airy anime. About 13 s. It follows prompts loosely, so name the hair colour and check the details.

All times are from a 12 GB RTX 4070 Ti.

Good to know:
- CocoaMix put a small made-up print on a plain hoodie on all four test seeds, so check clothing.
- Put every word you want on a MOSS picture in quotes. All three new MOSS cards spelled a two-line test poster right on all three seeds, but Serenity can hide a word behind a figure.
- MOSS-FET did not get a card: it added little in our test, and its author calls it an experimental first test.
- Wulver, Kromoss and Canon UltraReal from the same list already work: the first two have cards, and Canon UltraReal is a LoRA you can add from Manage LoRAs.
- Please credit the authors of AniMOSSity, Gock So and Osorubeshi when you share what you make; their licences ask for it.
