Thanks for the list! All five now have cards. They reach the Preset Store with the next sync; update the packs to get them.

**New cards**
- **MOSS-Image Finetunes:** MOSS Ad Infinitum Press (Classic). It needs the same engine as the other MOSS cards.
- **Anima Art & Styles:** Fn-Moment Anima v4.1
- **Krea 2 Finetunes:** Ray Artshoot Krea 2 v6
- **Krea 2 Anime:** Krea 2 Anime UWU v2.0
- **Krea 2 Art & Styles:** GodAgo v3 + 3ho Anime LoRA

A few notes:
- **GodAgo** on its own came out almost the same as stock Krea 2 in our test. The look on its page comes from the author's godago 3ho LoRA, so the card has that LoRA built in: backlit 90s cel anime, and darker, filmic photos. The LoRA makes it slower, about 47 seconds a picture on a 12 GB card. You can still add your own LoRAs on top.
- **Ad Infinitum** turns period scenes into posters without any style words. For modern things, start the prompt with "a vintage lithograph advertising poster of", and put any words you want on it in quotes. About 26 seconds a picture.
- **Fn-Moment** is the V4.1 file from the page you linked. It runs Euler at 30 steps, because the author's DPM++ 2M SDE smeared cloth and skin in our test. About 24 seconds a picture.
- **Ray Artshoot v6** frames wider and fills in more of the scene than stock Krea 2. Anime prompts come out as painted, semi-real pictures. About 32 seconds a picture.
- **UWU:** the author recommends a different text encoder, but it made little difference in our test, so the card uses the one the other Krea 2 cards share and there's nothing extra to download. About 26 seconds a picture.
