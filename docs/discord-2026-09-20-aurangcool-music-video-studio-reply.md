The full Studio you describe (analyse the song, storyboard it, render every section, stitch it) is an app feature, so
that part is Sharmystic's call. But one piece of it just landed as a community pack, and it is the piece I fought with
longest on my own music video: the **performance shots**.

**MiniMax H3 Music Video Chain** (3 cards): upload your song, pick the start offset, add a picture of your singer, and
you get one continuous singing shot of 14, 21 or 27 seconds, rendered as 2, 3 or 4 clips in a single job.

- Your song is locked into every clip, so the finished file carries your **original track**, not a re-generated one, and
  the mouth follows it. Leave the offset on an intro and the singer waits, then comes in on the first lyric.
- Each clip continues from the previous clip's latent, not from saved frames, so there is no visible cut and the look
  does not drift. That was the wall I kept hitting with 20-second sections: every new section re-invented the exposure
  and the framing.
- Different section of the song = same card, different start offset. So a verse, a chorus and a bridge are three runs
  with the same singer picture.

What it does not do: it is MiniMax H3, not LTX 2.5; 480p for now; it suits a singer performing to camera, not action;
and it does not plan or assemble anything. You still cut the sections together yourself, and B-roll comes from other
cards. If you want lip sync on LTX 2.5 specifically, the LTX 2.5 Lip-Sync (A2V) pack does single shots to your audio.

About 3 minutes per clip on a 12 GB card. Zip: https://github.com/michaeldune/simpligen-presets/releases/tag/packs-latest
