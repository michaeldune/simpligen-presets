**Two new packs from the 2026-11-09 request** (thanks for the list!)

**Pie Models** (8 cards)
Pie Models has 19 flavours, and most of the anime ones look almost the same. I tried all of them side by side and kept the eight that look clearly different:
- Anime: Apple Pie v2, Nutella, Jam, Cherry and Raspberry
- Semi-real: Mango, Key Lime and Impossible

Each flavour is its own checkpoint of about 7 GB.

**Illustrious Anime Mixes** (5 cards)
- **SilvermoonMix v6 V-pred:** soft, pale and airy. As the author recommends, it starts with no negative prompt.
- **JANKU v7.77:** bright and clean, with thicker lines. For a man, add `male focus` next to `1boy`.
- **dims2 Personal Merge v3.0:** bright, glossy cel shading.
- **ReedXXX v16:** warm colour and golden light. Name the outfit in your prompt, or it tends to draw very little clothing.
- **DREAMBOX v4.5:** often a flat, bold-outlined poster look. Its Civitai page lists over 100 style trigger words.

Plant Milk Walnut, A-mix and Red Lily came out very close to One Obsession v24, which is already in Illustrious Art & Styles, so they didn't get cards. Pie Models Anima was already in: Cream v2, now in Anima Art & Styles.

All of these are in the Preset Store now.
